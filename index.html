Portfolio Case Study
Intranet Migration
Novartis Pharmaceuticals · Basel, Switzerland

Many sites.
One standard.
Designed with precision.

Novartis Pharmaceuticals' public sites and internal intranets had grown independently across teams and regions — each with its own templates, permissions, and read on the brand. I led the UX for a full migration into one governed system, precise down to the pixel.

The Challenge

Every team at Novartis had built its own corner of the intranet — its own templates, its own read on the brand, its own rules for who could see what. A migration meant reconciling all of it into a single, governed system: masterpages and web parts built to exact brand standards, and a clear structure for who had access to what, without slowing any one team down.

My Role
